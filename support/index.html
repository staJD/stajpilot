What to include in your email

The more of this you include, the faster the issue can be diagnosed:

  • Your device model
  • iOS, iPadOS, or Android version
  • Your KEMPER PROFILER model (Player, Stage, Head, or Rack), MK1/MK2 where applicable, and firmware version
  • The USB cable you're using
  • Whether you're using an OTG adapter
  • Bluetooth pedal model, if applicable
  • The exact order of steps that led to the issue
  • A photo or short video of the screen, if possible

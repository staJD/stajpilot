StajPilot

A stage-ready display and MIDI controller app for the KEMPER PROFILER Player — on your phone or tablet.

StajPilot turns an iPhone, iPad, or Android device into a touch display for your KEMPER PROFILER Player: rig name, effect status, tuner, tap tempo, and bank/rig navigation, connected directly over USB MIDI.

Setup guide iPhone & iPad → USB MIDI connection, OTG notes, troubleshooting Setup guide Android → USB MIDI, Bluetooth MIDI pedals, troubleshooting
Support Privacy Policy

What StajPilot shows

  • 8 effect blocks, shown and touch-controlled
  • 5 Performance Slots
  • Bank navigation
  • Rig name display
  • Amp info display
  • Gain display
  • Tempo display and Tap Tempo
  • Morph status display
  • Needle Tuner
  • Strobe Tuner
  • Song Mode with custom song names
  • Slot Name and Sub Name
  • Demo Mode
  • Landscape layout built for stage visibility

No account, sign-in, subscription, or internet connection is required to use StajPilot. It connects directly to your KEMPER PROFILER Player over USB MIDI.

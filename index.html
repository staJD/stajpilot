STAJ mode / On stage

Your songs. Your names. Your performance.

Song Mode works with Player banks and the Performance slots of the larger PROFILER models. Keep the hardware connection, and give each song and slot names that make sense to you.

Select. Long-press. Edit.

Browse up to 125 songs in Song Select, with 25 shown at a time. Long-press a song to edit it directly. Multilingual names and emoji are stored locally, without extra name queries to the Profiler.

Edit on your computer, too

The web Song Editor lets you edit your exported Song file, organize song and slot names, and manage amp-image assignments. Export the result and import it back into the app. Your original Profiler rig names are not replaced.

New in Stage 2

Custom Tuner. Your tuning reference.

Save three custom profiles and name them your way. Set an offset for each open string, E A D G B E, from -15.0 to +15.0 cents in 0.1-cent steps.

Custom tuning applies to Needle mode. Keep Standard tuning when you want it, choose the cents display precision, or use the full-screen tuner for a larger view. Strobe remains available for Standard tuning.

Before you edit in PILOT mode

Please read before your first edit.

To keep your changes, press STORE on the KEMPER itself.

  • Set your device first. Select the correct Player, MK1, or MK2 profile in Settings. Player users must also select the correct Level.
  • Validation is ongoing. Extensive checks have been completed, but not every value has been fully verified. Some displayed values may still differ from the Profiler.
  • Avoid editing from both places at once. PILOT combines incoming MIDI with targeted queries and polling to reduce unnecessary device load. It is not fully bidirectional; avoid adjusting the Profiler's own controls while editing in PILOT mode.
  • Help us verify the details. If a value differs by more than 1%, or a control behaves incorrectly, email sk5054@hotmail.com with screenshots and a detailed explanation.

StajPilot communicates with the Profiler through USB MIDI. It does not intercept proprietary, non-MIDI communication protocols.

Support Privacy Policy

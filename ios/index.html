Prologue

I'm a guitar player.

While using the KEMPER PROFILER, I was deeply frustrated that I had to control 625 rigs without a display.

I simply built this to make things less inconvenient for myself.

Later, I released that work publicly as firmware, supported only by donations.

After that, I went on to develop StajPilot for iOS and Android to make it even more accessible.

The iOS version is far more sensitive to cables and connectors. This is an area StajPilot cannot control.

I tested about 30 cables and a range of OTG connectors. It would be great if every cable worked properly, but that was not the case. That is not a problem on the app's side.

Of the cables I tested, every one except a single cable had worked without any trouble on Android and on the hardware version of StajPilot.

The official release was delayed while I tracked down why some cables fail to connect or drop MIDI messages.

I have now identified the cause and reduced the instability seen with some cables.

Even so, it is not possible to prevent every cable-related error.

I have done my best so that every guitar player can use this app reliably.

StajPilot for iPhone & iPad

Connect over wired USB MIDI to your KEMPER PROFILER Player. No account, no internet connection, no pairing.

How to connect

  1. Get a USB cable that supports data (not a charge-only cable).
  2. Connect your iPhone or iPad to the USB-B port on the KEMPER PROFILER Player.
  3. Open StajPilot.
  4. Once MIDI data is received, the app switches from the waiting screen to the main display automatically.

About OTG / USB Host adapters

  • A compatible USB Host or OTG adapter is recommended — it helps the device recognize its USB role correctly and improves connection reliability.
  • Charge-only cables will not work — the cable must support data.
  • If USB role detection behaves differently depending on cable orientation or the order things are plugged in, use an OTG adapter instead of a direct cable.

Troubleshooting

Stuck on the "Waiting for Device" screen

Confirm the cable supports data, confirm it's connected to the Player's USB-B port, and confirm the Player is powered on. Try unplugging and reconnecting the cable after fully closing StajPilot (see below).

Shows "USB Connected" but no MIDI info appears

This usually means a data connection exists but the Player hasn't started sending MIDI data yet. Check the Player's firmware version, and try reconnecting the cable.

Reopening the app from the background doesn't reconnect

Fully close StajPilot from the app switcher, disconnect the USB cable, then reconnect it and reopen the app.

Another MIDI app is holding the USB connection

Close any other app that may be using MIDI over USB, then reopen StajPilot.

The cable or adapter isn't recognized

Try a different data-capable USB cable, or add a USB Host/OTG adapter between the cable and the device.

General reset steps

  1. Close StajPilot completely (remove it from the app switcher).
  2. Disconnect the USB cable from either end.
  3. Confirm the KEMPER PROFILER Player is powered on and its firmware is up to date.
  4. Reconnect the USB cable.
  5. Reopen StajPilot.

Still stuck? Contact Support with your device model, iOS/iPadOS version, Player firmware version, and the cable/adapter you're using.
